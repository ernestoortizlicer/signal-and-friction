# 🏛️ SIGNAL & FRICTION: PRICING AND SERVICES ARCHITECTURE

This document establishes the commercial framework, service definitions, deliverables, and strategic pricing rationales for both Signal & Friction delivery segments, certification licensing tiers, and à la carte add-ons.

---

## 1. SEGMENT A: DONE-FOR-YOU (DFY) — HIGH-TICKET COHORT
*   **Target Audience:** High-growth B2B SaaS platforms ($50k - $250k+ MRR).
*   **Value Prop:** Complete diagnostic isolation, direct code intervention, and ongoing performance monitoring backed by a results-based guarantee.

| Phase | Deliverable | Price (USD) | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beta Diagnostic** | Clinical diagnostic report isolating the primary conversion bottleneck + 10-min Loom video walkthrough + high-fidelity annotated interface mockup. | **$2,000** | High-prestige entry fee. Filters out low-intent leads. A 20% conversion lift on a $100k MRR business yields $20,000 in immediate value, making a $2k entrance fee highly ROI-positive. |
| **Intervention** | Direct implementation of the refactored code/workflow on the client's staging branch + live conversion tracking dashboard (PostHog/Stripe) + 10-min Loom code walkthrough. | **$3,000** | Direct developer implementation. SaaS founders pay for speed and certainty. De-risked by the Results-Based Guarantee. |
| **Monitoring** | 30-day post-deploy KPI monitoring + weekly Loom performance summaries + minor conversion adjustments. | **$2,500/mo** | Recurring optimization revenue. Ensures the fix holds and client receives compounding value. |
| **Expansion** | Repeat diagnostic and intervention protocol targeting the secondary checkout or onboarding friction point. | **$2,000** | Cross-selling second-stage optimization once trust and ROI are established in Phase 1. |
| **Autonomy Kit** | Full methodology transfer package + self-diagnosis checklists + final Loom handover for the client's internal growth team. | **$5,000** | One-time exit licensing fee. Allows the client to internalize the S&F framework and run ongoing diagnostics independently. |

*   **Total Potential LTV (Segment A):** **$14,500+**
