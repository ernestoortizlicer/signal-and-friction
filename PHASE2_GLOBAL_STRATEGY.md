## WHY URUGUAY WINS

### 1. Taxation
- **0% income tax on foreign-source income for 11 years** (Tax Holiday for new residents — Law 16.906 and IRNR reform 2022)
- After 11 years: Option to pay 7% flat on foreign passive income — still far better than Bulgaria's 14% effective rate
- Wyoming LLC invoices clients globally → income lands as Uruguayan resident's foreign income → **$0 tax in Uruguay for 11 years**
- No wealth tax. No capital gains tax on foreign assets. No inheritance tax.
- **Annual savings vs Bulgaria at $100K revenue:** ~$14,000 (14% effective rate difference)
- **Annual savings vs Bulgaria at $200K revenue:** ~$28,000

### 2. Climate
- Montevideo: Summers 25-30°C (Dec-Feb), Winters 8-15°C (Jun-Aug) — **mild temperate, similar to northern Spain**
- No extreme heat, no tropical humidity, no hurricanes
- 4 distinct seasons. Atlantic coast. Green landscape.
- Punta del Este: beach city 120km east, international, cosmopolitan

### 3. Language
- **Spanish** — native language. Zero adaptation required. Legal documents in Spanish. Tax filings in Spanish.
- Large expat community (Brazilian, Argentine, European). English common in business circles.

### 4. Safety
- **#1 or #2 safest country in South America** (Global Peace Index 2025)
- Montevideo = 1.5M people, European-scale urban safety
- Democratic since 1985 (after military dictatorship ended). Continuous rule of law.
- Transparency International ranks Uruguay #1 least corrupt in Latin America.

### 5. Culture
- 88% of population of European descent (Italian, Spanish, Basque, Welsh, German)
- Very progressive: first country in the Americas to legalize marijuana (2013), gay marriage (2013), state-controlled cannabis
- Education-centric society. High literacy rate (99%). Strong public universities.
- Called "the Switzerland of South America" — neutral, stable, rule of law
- Strong remote work infrastructure and growing digital nomad scene

### 6. Cost of Living
- Montevideo: 2-bedroom apartment rental: $700-1,200/month
- Restaurants (good): $8-15/meal
- Private healthcare: $80-150/month (FONASA or private mutualista)
- Total monthly budget for comfortable lifestyle: $2,000-3,000
- **At $15K MRR, Uruguay enables extreme savings rate (70-80%)**

## RESIDENCY PATH

### Option A — Physical Presence (Simplest)
1. Enter Uruguay as tourist (90 days, renewable)
2. Apply for residency (cedula) — requires: passport, apostilled birth certificate, clean criminal record, proof of income ($1,500+/month)
3. Spend 183+ days in Uruguay in calendar year
4. Register as "Residente Fiscal" with DGI (tax authority)
5. Submit "Declaración de Ingresos del Exterior" to claim 0% on foreign income
6. **Timeline: 6-12 months for permanent residency. Tax holiday starts from day of residency application.**

### Option B — Real Estate Investment (Faster, more stable)
1. Purchase property ≥ $380,000 USD in Uruguay
2. Combined with 60 days of physical presence per year = residency granted
3. More secure (no annual 183-day requirement)
4. Property in Punta del Este or Montevideo Pocitos area = good investment

**Recommendation for Ernesto:** Start with Option A (183 days). Test Uruguay lifestyle. After 2 years and income is stable, evaluate Option B.
