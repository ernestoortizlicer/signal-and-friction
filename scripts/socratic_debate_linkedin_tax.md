## SECTION 2: ZERO-TAX DESTINATION DEBATE

### 2.1 DESTINATION COMPARISON
*   **Global Tax Strategist:** Bulgaria is excellent for short-term capital accumulation (10% tax, low costs). However, for long-term zero-tax, Hong Kong is superior to Dubai or Singapore. Why? Hong Kong has a strict territorial tax system. Offshore income (consulting global clients outside HK) is taxed at **0%**. There is **0% dividend tax**, **0% withholding tax**, and **0% capital gains tax**. 
*   **Longevity Economist:** Hong Kong has high political stability, world-class banking (HSBC, Standard Chartered), and a high quality of life. The cost of living is high but manageable compared to Singapore.
*   **Global Expansion Hacker:** Dubai (UAE) has a 0% tax rate in Free Zones, but local corporate banking is slow, and invoice validation can trigger audits. Furthermore, Dubai has lower B2B SaaS prestige compared to Hong Kong or Singapore. Singapore has a 17% corporate tax rate, which is not zero-tax, and living expenses are the highest globally. Georgia has a 1% tax, but is not Stripe-native and lacks prestige.
*   **Rankings Consensus:**
    1.  **Bulgaria (Short-Term: 2026-2027)** — Capital accumulation, residency for Spanish citizen is trivial.
    2.  **Hong Kong (Long-Term: 2028+)** — 0% territorial tax on foreign income, premier banking, Stripe-native, highest prestige.
    3.  **UAE (Dubai) (Long-Term Alternative)** — 0% tax, easy visa, but higher banking friction and lower SaaS prestige.
