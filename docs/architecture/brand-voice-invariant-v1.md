# Signal & Friction Brand Voice Invariant v1

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-08-13
Owner: Ernesto Ortiz Licer

## Decision

Signal & Friction is currently operated by one person: Ernesto Ortiz Licer.

When Signal & Friction speaks in the provider's own voice, use first-person singular language: `I`, `me`, and `my`.

A neutral third-person reference to `Signal & Friction` is also allowed when it is clearer than first person.

Do not use `we`, `us`, `our`, `we're`, `we've`, `we'll`, `our team`, or `the team` to describe Signal & Friction personnel while the business is operated by one person.

## Purpose

This is a truthfulness invariant, not a tone preference. Public copy must not imply staff, review layers, or organizational depth that does not exist.

## Scope

Apply this rule to public pages, service explanations, client-facing messages, generated commercial communications, and any reusable public copy authority.

Do not mechanically rewrite third-party quotations, customer statements, generic descriptions of external groups, code comments, or historical records that need to remain verbatim for provenance.

## Examples

Incorrect: `We inspect the available evidence.`

Correct: `I inspect the available evidence.`

Incorrect: `Our team will review the context.`

Correct: `I will review the context.`

Neutral and allowed: `Signal & Friction has not recorded this state yet.`

## Architecture rule

Reusable public claims should live in canonical copy/config authorities rather than being duplicated across pages. First-person voice never relaxes evidence requirements: `I` must not be used to imply a fact that the system or evidence does not support.

## Revisit condition

Revisit this invariant only when Signal & Friction actually has additional human personnel whose role is material to delivery or client communication. AI agents, software tools, contractors used for isolated tasks, and third-party infrastructure do not by themselves justify a plural company voice.
