## WEEK 2 (Days 8–14): CONVERSATIONS

### The Shift

Week 1 was about volume. Week 2 is about conversations. Every reply is a pipeline opportunity. Your job is not to close on first reply — it's to extend the conversation one step.

**The Conversion Ladder:**
```
Cold DM/Email → Reply → Case Study Sent → Question Answered → Stripe Link Sent → Paid
```

Never skip a rung.

---

### Daily Rhythm (Week 2)

**Every morning (90 min):**
- [ ] Reply to all new DMs/emails first (within 2 hours of receipt, always)
- [ ] Send 5 new DMs (lower volume, higher personalization)
- [ ] Send 10 new emails

**Every afternoon (30 min):**
- [ ] Check Stripe for new payments
- [ ] Update pipeline statuses
- [ ] Send follow-ups from Week 1 (Sequence B messages)

**Week 2 targets:**
- [ ] 35+ DMs sent (cumulative)
- [ ] 30+ emails sent (cumulative)
- [ ] 3–5 active conversations in pipeline
- [ ] 1 Stripe link clicked
- [ ] First payment received (target: Day 10–14)

## THE MINDSET PROTOCOL

**Every morning, before opening LinkedIn:**
Read this:

> "I am not asking for charity. I am offering a results-based guarantee that makes the risk mathematically zero for the right client. My job is not to convince anyone. My job is to find the people who already have the problem I solve and make it easy for them to say yes. Rejection is not failure. Rejection is data. Every no is one step closer to the next yes."

**When a prospect goes cold:**
Don't chase. Execute Sequence B (3 touches maximum). Then move on. There are 50,000 B2B SaaS companies in the world. You need 500 of them.

**When revenue is slow:**
Don't change the offer. Don't lower the price. Change the targeting or the channel. The guarantee makes the offer unbeatable — the only variable is whether you're reaching the right people.
